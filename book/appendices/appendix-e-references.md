## Data Sources (Primary Statistical Agencies)

- **Bureau of Economic Analysis** (bea.gov): GDP, GDP by industry, GDP by state and metro, input-output accounts, international transactions.
- **Bureau of Labor Statistics** (bls.gov): Current Employment Statistics, Consumer Price Index, Occupational Employment Statistics, productivity.
- **Census Bureau** (census.gov): Economic Census, County Business Patterns, Statistics of U.S. Businesses, population estimates, trade statistics.
- **Federal Reserve / FRED** (fred.stlouisfed.org): Monetary data, Financial Accounts (Z.1), macroeconomic time series.
- **Congressional Budget Office** (cbo.gov): Budget and Economic Outlook, long-term fiscal projections.
- **Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services** (cms.gov): National Health Expenditure Accounts.
- **Energy Information Administration** (eia.gov): Energy production, consumption, and price data.
- **U.S. Department of Agriculture / Economic Research Service** (ers.usda.gov): Farm income, cash receipts, food system data.
- **Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation** (fdic.gov): Bank financial data, Quarterly Banking Profile.
